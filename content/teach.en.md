I teach mathematics at university and work on popularising it: I help edit the
children's magazine *Kvantik* and compose olympiad problems.

## University courses

I run electives on algebraic geometry at the Faculty of Computer Science (HSE)
and at MIPT. At HSE I also teach first-year Applied Mathematics and Computer
Science students the courses "Linear Algebra and Geometry" and "Advanced
Algebra".

## Popularising mathematics

### Kvantik

I am one of the editors of [Kvantik](https://kvantik.com/), a monthly magazine
for curious schoolchildren published by MCCME.

- compiler of the *Kvantik almanac* collections (incl. volumes 16 and 17);
- I check submissions for the [monthly Kvantik contests](https://konkurs.kvantik.com/).

### Olympiad problems

I compose problems for mathematical olympiads. Some are collected on
[problems.ru (author #476)](https://problems.ru/view_by_author.php?author=476):
combinatorics, number theory, plane geometry and sequences. A few appeared in
the LXXII Moscow Mathematical Olympiad (2009).
